FAQ

Common questions

Is Peelr really free to use?+

Yes. Peelr runs entirely in your browser, so there's no per-image server cost — you can remove backgrounds and download full-resolution PNGs with no account and no watermark.

Does my photo get uploaded to a server?+

No. Processing happens locally on your device using your browser's canvas engine. Your image never leaves your computer.

What image formats does Peelr support?+

You can upload JPG, PNG, or WEBP files. Results are downloaded as transparent PNG files.

Will it work on photos with busy or textured backgrounds?+

The current engine is tuned for solid or near-solid backgrounds — which covers most product photos, ID photos, and studio headshots. Support for complex, multi-color backgrounds is on our roadmap.

Is there a maximum image size?+

You can upload images up to 25MB. Very large images are automatically scaled for fast processing while preserving quality.
